# Intelligent Linking

Intelligent linking is how RAP gives communications business context. It helps the platform understand which account, customer relationship, or related business record an email or meeting belongs to.

## What It Accomplishes

Linking turns raw communication history into usable product context.

- It connects communications to the right business records.
- It gives downstream facts and artifacts a business home instead of leaving them attached only to a message or transcript.
- It lets RAP maintain continuity as conversations evolve across email threads and meetings.

## Relationship Meaning

Not every external company plays the same role in a conversation. RAP can distinguish between different kinds of business relationships, such as:

- **Client** when the communication is directly about the customer relationship
- **Partner** when another company is acting within the customer process
- **Competitor** when a competitive context matters to the business conversation

This helps RAP interpret the same communication differently depending on who is involved and why they are involved.

## What The Agent Should Know

Linking is meant to be useful, not aggressive.

- RAP prefers broader context over a wrong specific context.
- A communication may be linked only to an account if the product cannot justify a narrower record-level association.
- A communication may relate to more than one plausible record when the business context is genuinely shared.

## Continuity Across Conversations

Business context often continues across multiple communications rather than resetting every time.

- Related emails in the same thread can carry forward earlier context.
- Meetings can continue a conversation that started in email.
- Later messages can narrow, reinforce, or change the apparent business context.

This is why RAP treats linking as part of an ongoing conversation, not just a one-time match on a single message.

## Why Linking Matters

Linking sits underneath much of RAP's value.

- Facts inherit business context from linking.
- Meeting and CRM artifacts become more meaningful when they are tied to the right records.
- User-facing summaries and predictions are more trustworthy when RAP understands where a communication belongs.

For the agent, the main takeaway is simple: linking is RAP's way of turning communication activity into business context.
